FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AMC?

Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) is a service contract covering maintenance, support, and repairs of IT hardware/equipment.

What hardware do you support under your AMC packages?

We offer AMC support for Servers, laptops, Desktop computers, firewalls, printers.

Do you offer on-site support under your AMCs?

Yes, our engineers can provide you with on-site and remote support depending on the type of AMC you choose.

What is the difference between Comprehensive and Non-Comprehensive AMCs?

Comprehensive AMC package comprises labour, maintenance, and hardware replacement (based on agreement). Non-Comprehensive AMC comprises labour, and maintenance but not hardware replacemen.

Do you offer AMCs in all of India?

Yes, we have PAN India AMC service with scheduled maintenance services and technical support.
